nbody.py adapts NVIDIA Warp's tile N-body example, Copyright (c) 2025
NVIDIA CORPORATION & AFFILIATES. All rights reserved., licensed under Apache License 2.0.
The tiled algorithm is described by Lars Nyland, Mark Harris and Jan Prins
in GPU Gems 3, chapter 31, Fast N-Body Simulation with CUDA.

LICENSE-APACHE-2.0.txt. They are not relicensed under Tack's BSD license.
